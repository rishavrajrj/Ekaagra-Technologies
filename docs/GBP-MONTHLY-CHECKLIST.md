# Google Business Profile (GBP) — Monthly Maintenance Checklist

To maintain high visibility in Google’s Local Map Pack in Motihari and East Champaran, the business owner should complete this 15-minute checklist on the 1st of every month.

---

## 1. Profile Integrity Check (5 Minutes)

- [ ] **Business Name:** Ensure it remains strictly `Ekaagra Technologies` (never add keywords like *"Best Web Developer"* as Google's automated scanners suspend profiles for name tampering).
- [ ] **Primary Category:** Confirm it is set to `Website designer`.
- [ ] **Secondary Categories:** Confirm `Software company` and `Internet marketing service` are present.
- [ ] **Service Areas:** Verify Motihari, East Champaran, Bettiah, Muzaffarpur, and Bihar are listed.
- [ ] **Website URL:** Ensure link points to `https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site/`.
- [ ] **Appointment Link:** Ensure link points to `https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site/get-quote`.

---

## 2. Customer Reviews Management (5 Minutes)

- [ ] **Check New Reviews:** Review all ratings and comments received in the preceding 30 days.
- [ ] **Public Response:** Respond to 100% of reviews using the professional response templates from `docs/REVIEW-STRATEGY.md`.
  - *Rule:* Acknowledge the client by name and reference the type of project (e.g. school web portal, custom software) naturally.
- [ ] **New Review Requests:** For any client projects completed during the month, send the pre-formatted WhatsApp review request link within 48 hours of final launch.

---

## 3. Fresh Content & Visual Updates (5 Minutes)

- [ ] **Upload 1–2 Fresh Photos:**
  - Screenshots of newly deployed client websites or dashboards.
  - Authentic photo of the developer workstation or client planning session.
  - *Rule:* Never upload generic stock photography or foreign office buildings.
- [ ] **Publish 1 Google Business Post ("Add Update"):**
  - **Topic Example:** *"We recently deployed the new online admissions portal for Roshani Public School in Bihar. Learn how modern CBSE-compliant websites streamline school administration: https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site/school-website-development-motihari"*
  - **Button:** *Learn more* linking to the relevant service or case study page.

---

## 4. Performance Insights Review

- [ ] Check **GBP Performance Tab**:
  - **Searches:** Total times profile appeared in local search results.
  - **Website Clicks:** How many visitors clicked through to `ekaagratechnologies.site`.
  - **Call Clicks:** Direct phone calls triggered from the local profile.
- [ ] Log these numbers in [`docs/MONTHLY-SEO-REPORT.md`](file:///d:/Antigravity%20Projects/Ekaagra%20Technologies/docs/MONTHLY-SEO-REPORT.md).
