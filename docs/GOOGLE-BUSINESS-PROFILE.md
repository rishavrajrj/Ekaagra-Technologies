# Google Business Profile (GBP) — Complete Setup & Local Optimization Guide

## 1. Business Model Definition: Service Area Business (SAB)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Storefront vs Service Area Business**:
> Ekaagra Technologies operates as a modern digital software and website studio. Unless you maintain a staffed, dedicated commercial office storefront in Motihari with visible exterior signage open to walk-in foot traffic during posted hours, **Google guidelines require creating the profile as a Service Area Business (SAB)**.
> 
> Declaring a residential or virtual address as a public storefront risks immediate suspension by Google's automated verification systems. By selecting **Service Area Business**, you hide any private residential address while displaying your full geographic service coverage across Motihari and Bihar on Google Maps.

## 6. Profile Verification Process

1. Go to [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business) and begin profile creation.
2. Enter your mailing address for verification (Google may verify via video recording of your workspace/tools, phone SMS, or postal postcard).
3. If video verification is requested:
   - Record a short, continuous video showing your street name/landmark in Motihari, your business workspace (computer/laptop logged into the Ekaagra code editor/domain registrar), and business cards or registered invoices.
4. Once verified, add the public website URL: `https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site`.
5. Set up appointment link: `https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site/get-quote`.
