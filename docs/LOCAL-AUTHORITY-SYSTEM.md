# Local Authority Operating System — 4-Week Monthly Cadence

This document establishes the recurring operational rhythm for Ekaagra Technologies to build enduring local brand recognition, search engine authority, and high-trust customer conversion in Motihari, East Champaran, and Bihar.

---

## 1. The 4-Week Recurring Cadence

```text
Week 1: Google Business Profile & Profile Accuracy
  │
Week 2: Customer Proof & Review Acquisition
  │
Week 3: External Authority & Natural Links
  │
Week 4: Content Depth & Commercial Page Optimization
```

---

## Week 1: Google Business Profile & Map Pack Health (15–20 Minutes)

- **Audit Profile Accuracy:**
  - Verify that the business name remains strictly `Ekaagra Technologies` with zero keyword stuffing.
  - Confirm service area includes Motihari, East Champaran, Bettiah, Muzaffarpur, and Bihar.
- **Review Responses:**
  - Check for any new customer reviews received over the past month.
  - Post a personalized, polite public response acknowledging the specific project type.
- **Upload Fresh Visual Proof:**
  - Upload 1–2 authentic screenshots of recent client project launches or developer workspace setups.
  - Never upload foreign stock photography.
- **Publish a GBP Update:**
  - Share a 2-paragraph update highlighting a recent case study or educational guide with a link back to `https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site/`.

---

## Week 2: Customer Proof & Review Acquisition (30 Minutes)

- **Identify Recent Deliverables:**
  - Review all client projects deployed or reaching staging milestones in the last 30 days.
- **Outreach for Honest Feedback:**
  - Contact the client via WhatsApp using the outreach template from [`docs/REVIEW-STRATEGY.md`](file:///d:/Antigravity%20Projects/Ekaagra%20Technologies/docs/REVIEW-STRATEGY.md).
  - Explicitly ask for honest feedback — never script keywords or pressure clients.
- **Update Case Studies:**
  - For major projects, update `src/lib/data.ts` with newly completed modules, screenshots, and live URLs.

---

## Week 3: External Authority & Natural Links (45 Minutes)

- **Client Footer Attribution:**
  - For newly launched client sites where permission was granted, verify the subtle attribution link:
    `Website by <a href="https://www.ekaagratechnologies.site/">Ekaagra Technologies</a>`.
- **Educational & Community Outreach:**
  - Reach out to 1 local school trustee or merchant association offering a complimentary website security/CBSE compliance review.
- **Update Link Building Pipeline:**
  - Log active conversations and verified links in [`docs/LINK-BUILDING-PIPELINE.md`](file:///d:/Antigravity%20Projects/Ekaagra%20Technologies/docs/LINK-BUILDING-PIPELINE.md).

---

## Week 4: Content Depth & Commercial Page Optimization (45 Minutes)

- **Search Console Query Review:**
  - Filter queries by Impressions > 100 and Average Position 4.0–20.0.
  - If a commercial page has low CTR, refine the title tag or introductory value proposition.
- **Substantive Content Refresh:**
  - Review 1 existing blog post (e.g. `/blog/website-development-cost-in-motihari-bihar`) to ensure pricing figures and tech stacks reflect current market standards.
  - Update `dateModified` in `src/lib/blog-data.ts` only when substantive content changes were made.
- **Log Performance Data:**
  - Record the month's metrics using [`docs/MONTHLY-SEO-REPORT.md`](file:///d:/Antigravity%20Projects/Ekaagra%20Technologies/docs/MONTHLY-SEO-REPORT.md).
