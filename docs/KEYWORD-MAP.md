## 5. Canonicalization & Cannibalization Prevention Rules

1. **Never create redundant landing pages** with slight variations (e.g. do NOT create `/website-developer-motihari` alongside `/website-development-motihari`).
2. **Every blog post must contextually funnel traffic** to its designated commercial destination.
3. **Always use canonical tags** to resolve non-www and duplicate URL parameters.
