## 3. Ethical Review Acquisition Playbook

Online reviews are among the top 3 ranking factors for local Google search results. Follow this ethical framework:

### Golden Rules
1. **Never buy or fake reviews.** Google's spam detection algorithm flags sudden bursts of reviews from unverified accounts.
2. **Never offer incentives** (e.g., discounts or gifts) in exchange for 5-star ratings.
3. **Ask genuine clients** immediately following a successful launch milestone or successful staging demo.

### Client Review Request Script (WhatsApp / Email)
```
Hello [Client Name],

Thank you for trusting Ekaagra Technologies with the development of the [Project Name, e.g. Roshani Public School portal]. We loved bringing this project to life!

If you were satisfied with our development process, turnaround speed, and communication, would you mind taking 60 seconds to share your honest review on our official Google profile?

It helps other local schools and businesses in Motihari discover our services:
👉 [Insert Your Google Review Shortlink here]

Thank you for your partnership!
— The Ekaagra Technologies Team
```

### How to Respond to All Reviews
- Respond to every review within 48 hours.
- Mention specific details naturally (e.g., *"Thank you Principal Sharma, it was a pleasure engineering the admission portal and notice board for your school in Motihari!"*).
