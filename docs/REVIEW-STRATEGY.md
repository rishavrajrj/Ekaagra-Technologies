# Ethical Client Review & Testimonial Acquisition Strategy

## 1. Ethical Standards & Google Policy Compliance

Google’s review guidelines explicitly penalize manipulated, incentivized, or fabricated customer feedback. Ekaagra Technologies adheres strictly to these principles:

### 🚫 Strictly Forbidden
- **Never purchase fake reviews** from freelance networks or review agencies.
- **Never write reviews internally** using employee, friend, or personal alternate accounts.
- **Never offer financial incentives, discounts, or gifts** in exchange for 5-star ratings (this violates FTC guidelines and Google policy).
- **Never script client reviews** with copy-pasted keyword phrases.
- **Never review-gate** (filtering negative reviews through private forms while funneling only positive reviews to Google).

### ✅ Approved Practice
- Ask **genuine clients** who have experienced our development process, staging reviews, and final deployments.
- Collect reviews on our official **Google Business Profile** and client website case studies.
- Allow clients to express their authentic voice, experience, and feedback.

---

## 2. Ideal Timing: When to Request Reviews

Timing is critical for high conversion rates when requesting client feedback:

1. **At Private Staging Approval (Best Emotional Moment):**  
   When the client tests their completed website or school ERP on the private staging link and expresses excitement with the design and speed.
2. **At Public Domain Launch:**  
   Immediately following final DNS pointing and official go-live when the site is visible on Google.
3. **At 30-Day Milestone Check-in:**  
   When conducting the post-launch review and reviewing initial parent or customer inquiry data.

## 4. How to Generate Your Google Review Shortlink

1. Open your verified **Google Business Profile** manager.
2. Look for the card titled **“Get more reviews”** or **“Ask for reviews”**.
3. Click the button to generate your unique shortlink (e.g. `https://g.page/r/.../review`).
4. Save this exact link and insert it into the outreach templates above.

## 5. Professional Review Response Playbook

Always respond to every review publicly within 48 hours. Responses demonstrate active customer care and reinforce local relevance naturally.

### Example Response to a Positive Review
```text
Thank you so much for the kind words, [Client Name]! It was a pleasure collaborating with the management of [School/Business Name] in Motihari to build your custom web platform. We appreciate your trust in Ekaagra Technologies and look forward to continuing our partnership!
```

### Example Response to Constructive Feedback
```text
Thank you for your valuable feedback, [Client Name]. We take operational improvements seriously. Our team has reached out directly to ensure all your questions regarding the new dashboard are thoroughly addressed. Thank you for partnering with Ekaagra Technologies.
```
