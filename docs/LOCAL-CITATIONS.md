# Legitimate Local Citations & Business Directory Playbook

## 1. Citation Strategy Philosophy: Quality > Quantity

Local SEO algorithms (especially Google's local pack) evaluate **NAP consistency** (Name, Address, Phone) across reputable online directories. 

> [!WARNING]
> **Avoid Directory Spam Networks**:
> Purchasing packages claiming *"500 local directory submissions for ₹1,999"* will severely damage your domain reputation. These services submit business details to dormant, spam-filled link farms that trigger automated algorithmic penalties.
> 
> Focus exclusively on **authoritative, verified platforms** that real business buyers in India actually utilize.
