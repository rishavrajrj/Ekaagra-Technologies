# White-Hat Authority Pipeline & Opportunity Management

## 1. Authority Acquisition Governance

Google's ranking algorithms evaluate genuine external brand mentions, editorial citations, and authentic business partnerships.

### Stage Definitions:
- `IDENTIFIED`: Opportunity discovered and verified for relevance and quality.
- `CONTACTED`: Formal outreach initiated via email or direct WhatsApp.
- `NEGOTIATING`: Terms, client consent, or content collaboration being finalized.
- `PUBLISHED`: Link or brand mention is live, indexable, and verified.
- `DECLINED`: Opportunity rejected due to quality concerns or non-response.

## 4. Monthly SEO Decision Tree

At the beginning of each calendar month, step through this 5-question tree:

```text
Question 1: Did organic impressions increase?
  ├── YES ──► Identify winning queries in GSC and reinforce supporting links.
  └── NO  ──► Inspect GSC indexation coverage, verify sitemap, and check robots.txt.

Question 2: Did organic clicks increase?
  ├── YES ──► Analyze which landing pages generated the clicks.
  └── NO  ──► Check Average CTR on striking-distance queries (test compelling meta titles).

Question 3: Did search rankings improve?
  ├── YES ──► Observe which topical cluster gained authority.
  └── NO  ──► Check competitor updates, search intent alignment, and content depth.

Question 4: Did qualified leads increase (WhatsApp / Quote)?
  ├── YES ──► Replicate successful conversion patterns across other service pages.
  └── NO  ──► Audit landing page UX, CTA visibility, pricing clarity, and mobile speed.

Question 5: Which content cluster is winning?
  └── Focus 80% of next month's effort on expanding the winning cluster.
```
