# White-Hat Local Authority & Link Building Pipeline

## 1. Outreach Philosophy: Real Relationships > Manufactured Links

Local search algorithms favor **relevance, community context, and genuine business relationships**. A single link from an active school portal in Bihar or an established local business is exponentially more valuable than hundreds of spam directory submissions.

## 3. High-Integrity Outreach Strategy Guidelines

1. **Client Attribution:** Only add footer links with explicit client consent. Never hide links or use keyword-stuffed anchor text. Use clean branded text (*"Website by Ekaagra Technologies"*).
2. **Educational Collaborations:** Offer free digital security, CBSE compliance, or website audits for local educational institutions in East Champaran in exchange for official partner citations.
3. **No Paid Bulk Links:** Reject all offers for *"1,000 links for ₹2,000"*, private blog networks (PBNs), automated forum blasts, or link farms. These trigger algorithmic spam penalties and permanent de-indexing.
